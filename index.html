What is ACADEX?

About us

ACADEX is a web-based student performance tracker built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Python. Teachers record marks and daily attendance, and ACADEX turns them into grades, attendance percentages and progress charts.

Marks and gradesScores by subject, graded A+ to F.
Daily attendanceMark present, late or absent in one tap.
Progress chartsSee how each student improves over time.
CSV exportDownload marks and attendance for reports.
Extra-curricularsMusic, dance, sports, clubs and the achievements that go with them.
